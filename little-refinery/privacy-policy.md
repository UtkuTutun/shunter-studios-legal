# Privacy Policy

Little Refinery, by Truss Studios

Effective date: 2026-11-25

Version 1

Türkçe: [Gizlilik Politikası](tr/gizlilik-politikasi.md)

Truss Studios ("we", "us") develops and publishes the game Little Refinery (the "game") and is the data controller for the personal data in this policy. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

## Data we use

**Offline play** needs no account. Your save file stays on your device.

**Guest account.** When online features are available, the game creates a guest account automatically. It uses a random device key created on your device (our server stores only a SHA-256 hash of it), an account ID (shown under Menu > Account), the device name (for example your phone model) and platform, and sign-in tokens (long-lived tokens are stored only as hashes). Session records show which device has your company open and when it was last seen, so that one device plays it at a time.

**Linked accounts.** If you link a platform account to your company when the game offers it (for example Google Play Games, Steam or Discord), we store that platform's account ID to sign you in. We never receive your password.

**Cloud save.** When you are online, the game uploads your save file, including the company name and emblem you chose, so you can restore it on another device. Older versions are kept for 30 days. We check uploads for cheating, such as more game time than real time allows. If an upload fails this check, your progress stops counting for leaderboards and consortiums, but you can keep playing. If you think this is a mistake, contact us.

**Moving to another device** uses a one-time 8-character link code, valid for 10 minutes and stored only as a hash. If both devices have a company, you keep one, and the other is archived for 30 days, then deleted.

**Leaderboards** show your company name and weekly score (completed orders) to other players.

**Consortiums** (player groups) involve the consortium's name, description and emblem, which other players can see; membership, join requests and roles; weekly contributions; chat messages (preset quick messages or your own text); mutes; and reports of inappropriate names or messages. Members see each other's company names, roles, contributions and messages; those who manage the consortium also see join requests with the applicant's in-game income. A consortium's name, emblem and order progress also appear in the weekly ranking of consortiums of a similar size. Each consortium keeps its last 200 messages. Writing your own messages requires accepting the chat rules, which forbid sharing personal details. We record when you accept them.

**Friends.** We process friend codes, friend requests, blocks, name reports and the profile your friends see: company name, emblem, title, consortium, in-game income, number of achievements and when you were last active.

**Purchases** are processed by Google Play on Android and by Steam on PC. We never see your payment details. The store gives us a purchase token or receipt, which we use to verify, deliver and restore the purchase.

**Rewarded ads (Android).** An ad appears only when you choose to watch one for a bonus. You can play the whole game without ads, and supporters can get the rewards without them. Ads come through an ad mediation SDK and its ad networks, which may collect device identifiers such as your advertising ID under their own privacy policies and your consent choices. Where the law requires consent for personalized ads (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks first. You can change your choice in the game's settings, and reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

**Server logs.** Our servers use IP addresses in logs and to limit requests, to keep the service secure and prevent abuse. Logs are kept for at most 30 days.

**Analytics.** No analytics or tracking is active. If we add opt-in analytics, we will update this policy before turning it on.

## Who receives data

- **Other players**, as described above.
- **Our cloud hosting provider** runs our servers and processes data only on our instructions.
- **Google Play and Steam** process purchases under their own privacy policies.
- **The ad mediation provider and ad networks** receive data on Android to serve ads. You can ask us which networks are active.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it.
- **A successor**, if the game or the studio is transferred. We will tell you in the game first.

Our servers may be in another country. For such transfers, we use the safeguards the law requires, such as standard contractual clauses or, for data from Türkiye, those in Article 9 of the KVKK.

Our community Discord server, our Steam pages and the store pages are run by other companies under their own privacy policies.

## Deleting your account

We keep your account data while your account exists. To delete your account:

1. Open Little Refinery and go to **Menu > Account > Delete account**.
2. Confirm.

The account stops syncing, and you can undo the deletion in the game for 30 days. After that, we permanently delete the account, its cloud saves and the data linked to it, such as scores, sessions, consortium membership, friends and reports you made.

What remains:

- The save file on your device. You can remove it by deleting the game's data, for example by uninstalling the game on Android.
- Chat messages you sent, until newer messages replace them.
- Reports other players made about you, as long as needed for moderation.
- Server logs, for at most 30 days.
- Purchase records that Google Play or Steam keep under their own policies.

If you can no longer open the game, write to the developer email address shown on the game's Google Play or Steam store page. Include details that help us find your account, such as your company name, platform and, if you have it, account ID. We may ask for more information to confirm that the account is yours.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data and get a copy, correct it, delete it, receive it in a portable format (including your cloud save), object to or restrict processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to a data protection authority, such as the one where you live in the EU or EEA, or the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK.

To use these rights, write to the developer email address shown on the store page, or delete your account in the game. As accounts have no email address or password, we may ask for details that confirm the account is yours. We answer free of charge within 30 days.

**Türkiye (KVKK).** Under Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK), Truss Studios is the data controller. We collect the data above by automated means through the game, our servers and the stores' receipts, for the purposes and on the legal bases above. Under Article 11, you have the right to:

- learn whether your data is processed, and request information about it;
- learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used for it;
- know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad who receive your data;
- ask for correction of incomplete or inaccurate data;
- ask for deletion or destruction under the conditions in Article 7;
- ask that recipients be told about a correction, deletion or destruction;
- object to a result against you that comes only from automated analysis;
- claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

If we reject your request, our answer is insufficient or we do not answer within 30 days, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu) within 30 days of our answer, and in any case within 60 days of your request.

**United States.** Depending on your state, you may also have the right to opt out of targeted advertising, "sale" or "sharing". We do not sell personal data for money, but personalized ads on Android may count as targeted advertising or "sharing". You can opt out in your device's ad settings, or through the game's ad choices where shown. We will not treat you differently for using your rights, and you can appeal a refusal by replying to us.

## Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13, including under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, and if we learn that one has used the online features, we delete that account's data. Contact us if you believe this has happened. In some countries, players under a certain age (up to 16 in the EU) need a parent's or guardian's permission to consent to personalized ads.

## Security

Connections to our servers are encrypted, device keys and long-lived tokens are stored only as hashes, and requests are rate limited. If a breach affects your data, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy. The effective date and version at the top show the current one, and we will tell you in the game about important changes before they take effect.

For questions and requests, write to the developer email address shown on the game's Google Play and Steam store pages. To delete your account, use **Menu > Account > Delete account** in the game.
